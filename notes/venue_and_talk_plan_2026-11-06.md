# Venue and talk plan

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Status:** Working plan; no venue contacted yet.

## Venue categories, in order of fit

| Venue | Fit | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSRN working paper | High | Account; PDF; abstract | Immediate, no endorsement; the natural home for a q-fin working paper |
| arXiv q-fin | High | Endorsement for a first submission | Categories `q-fin.PM`, `q-fin.ST`; the endorsement is the friction |
| University seminar | High | One adjacent professor; 20–30 minutes | The best feedback per hour; ask for a critique, not a slot |
| Local quant / data-science meetup | Medium | 10–12 minute talk | Good for distribution; record it if allowed |
| Workshops | Medium | A call that fits | Deadlines vary; do not chase a mismatch |
| Journals | Low (for now) | Peer review, months | Only after a replication and a DOI |

Do not pay for publication, and do not submit to a venue whose review you cannot
describe in one sentence.

## Submission checklist

- Title, 150-word abstract, and the DOI.
- The replication, not only the first sample.
- The code, committed tables, and verification logic. **Do not submit the
  snapshot**; it is not distributed.
- The disclosure: exploratory first sample; external replication (protocol dated 2026-11-05, freeze not independently verified) for the country sample;
  not investment advice.
- A one-page cover note that states the contribution and the limitation.

## Talk outline, twelve minutes

| Slide | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Most backtests are underpowered. Mine was." | 1 min |
| 2 | The question and the decision | 1 min |
| 3 | The design: seven methods, one component apart | 1 min |
| 4 | The result: 0.8538, +0.064, 0.20 bar | 2 min |
| 5 | The flaw: 0.478 observed / 0.72 true / 24% power | 2 min |
| 6 | The correction: paired, +0.064 [−0.011, 0.147], 0.20 excluded | 2 min |
| 7 | The replication: `B1` wins, the edge does not replicate | 2 min |
| 8 | The lesson: the comparator, not the correction | 1 min |

The talk is the paper's argument, not a tour of the repository.

## What not to do

- Do not submit the carousel as a paper.
- Do not add a second project to fill the wait.
